Privacy Policy — Aether: Gravity Inversion

Developer / Studio: Old & Rusty
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Local Data Storage

Aether: Gravity Inversion is an offline-first mobile game. All gameplay progress, level stars, high scores, unlocked cores/themes, and daily login streaks are stored locally on your device. We do not operate external user account servers or collect your personal name, physical address, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Services (Ads & In-App Purchases)

Our game integrates Google AdMob (solely for a Home Screen banner and 100% voluntary opt-in rewarded video ads) and Google Play Billing (for optional Gem pack purchases). These third-party SDKs may automatically collect and process standard device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID), coarse location/IP data, and crash diagnostics to serve ads, prevent fraud, and process transactions in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Deletion & Reset

Because all game progression data is stored locally on your device, you can permanently erase all saved game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings or uninstalling the application. You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your phone's Google Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
