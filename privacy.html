1. About Old & Rusty

Old & Rusty is an independent game development studio dedicated to crafting tactile, high-contrast physics, precision, and logic games for mobile devices. Our title, our games, exemplifies our focus on pure mechanical flow, tactile feedback, and instant responsiveness.

Our Core Engineering Philosophy: We believe everyone should be able to enjoy great games regardless of whether they own a brand-new flagship phone or an older, "rusty" system. Every title we publish is engineered with pre-allocated Zero-Garbage-Collection (Zero-GC) memory pools, offline-first architecture, and strict frame-rate optimization so mobile hardware runs cool, battery-efficient, and locked at 60 FPS.

2. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Old & Rusty ("we", "our", or "us") handles information inside the mobile application our games (the "App"). We respect your privacy and have engineered the App to operate with minimal data exposure and zero developer-operated cloud tracking servers.

2.1 Information We Do Not Collect

Old & Rusty does not require user account registration, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your real name, physical address, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or precise GPS location.

2.2 Locally Stored Game Progress

All gameplay progression—including unlocked stages, completion records, achievements, high scores, lives/energy regeneration timers, and custom user preferences—is stored strictly locally on your device using sandboxed local storage (such as localStorage or local app data containers). We operate zero external cloud databases holding your save states.

2.3 Children's Privacy

Our App is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us and we will promptly investigate and address it.

3. Data Collection, Third-Party SDKs & Security

While Old & Rusty operates zero external backend servers or proprietary telemetry APIs, the App integrates official platform SDKs provided by Google LLC to deliver verified operations, billing entitlements, and optional advertising:

SDK / Service Data Processed Purpose
Google AdMob SDK
(Google Mobile Ads)		 Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), coarse IP-based region, device model, ad interaction metrics, and crash diagnostics. Fulfilling voluntary, player-initiated Rewarded Video Ads for bonus lives and puzzle hints.
Google Play Billing & Licensing Transaction verification tokens, order IDs, SKU identifiers, and purchase entitlement status. Processing the optional VIP Package purchase (which doubles maximum life capacity and provides 5 hints per rewarded ad instead of 1) and verifying digital license ownership.
Local Device Storage Sandboxed key-value pairs, completion states, and cryptographic integrity checksums. Preventing save tampering and persisting offline stage progression and settings directly on device.

3.1 Opt-Out & Reset Controls

  • Advertising ID Reset: You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time by navigating to your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Complete Local Data Erasure: Because all gameplay data resides locally on your device, clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local save data.

For further details on how Google processes data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Terms & Conditions of Use

4.1 License to Use

Old & Rusty grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to download and play the App for personal entertainment. You may not decompile, reverse-engineer, modify, inject unauthorized scripts into, or redistribute the App binary.

4.2 In-App Purchases, VIP Package & Digital Items

All in-app purchases—specifically the optional VIP Package (which doubles maximum player life capacity and grants 5 puzzle hints instead of the standard 1 hint when watching rewarded ads)—are digital licensed enhancements with no real-world monetary value. These items are non-transferable, non-refundable outside standard Google Play refund policies, and cannot be redeemed for cash. Note: The VIP package does not modify ad frequency, as our titles feature zero pop-up and zero banner ads by default; optional rewarded video ads remain strictly player-initiated for voluntary reward claims.

4.3 Integrity & Anti-Tamper

The App may utilize local checksum verification and data integrity checks. Any attempt to alter local save files, bypass mechanics, or forge reward states will trigger an automated state baseline reset.

5. Fair-Play Advertising Disclosure

At Old & Rusty, we believe intrusive advertising ruins mobile gaming. We enforce a strict, transparent Fair-Play Ad Standard inside our games:

  • Zero Pop-Up Interstitials: We never interrupt your flow with unprompted full-screen interstitial video pop-ups between levels or unexpected crash pop-ups.
  • Zero In-Game Banners: The screen remains 100% clean and unobstructed, with zero banner ads across active gameplay and menus.
  • 100% Opt-In Rewarded Videos: Video ads only play when you voluntarily tap an ad reward button to claim extra lives or hints. Standard players receive 1 hint per reward; players with the VIP package receive doubled lives and 5 hints per reward.
