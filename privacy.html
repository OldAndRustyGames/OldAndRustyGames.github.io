1. About Old & Rusty

Old & Rusty is an independent game development studio dedicated to crafting tactile, high-contrast physics and logic games for mobile devices. Our flagship release, Aether: Gravity Inversion, combines 2.5D polarity-shift mechanics with over 100 precision puzzle chambers.

Our Core Engineering Philosophy: We believe everyone should be able to enjoy great games regardless of whether they own a brand-new flagship phone or an older, "rusty" system. Every title we publish is engineered with pre-allocated Zero-Garbage-Collection (Zero-GC) memory pools, offline-first architecture, and strict frame-rate optimization so low-end hardware runs cool and responsive.

2. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Old & Rusty ("we", "our", or "us") handles information inside the mobile application Aether: Gravity Inversion (the "App"). We respect your privacy and have engineered the App to operate with minimal data exposure and zero developer-operated cloud tracking servers.

2.1 Information We Do Not Collect

Old & Rusty does not require user account registration, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your real name, physical address, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or precise GPS location.

2.2 Locally Stored Game Progress

All gameplay progression—including unlocked levels, star ratings, high scores, heart regeneration timers, gem balances, equipped cores/themes, and user-locked voucher redemption states—is stored strictly locally on your device using cryptographically signed browser/WebView storage (localStorage). We have no remote access to your local save files.

2.3 Children's Privacy

Our App is a general-audience physics puzzle game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has inadvertently provided personal information via a support email, please contact us and we will immediately purge it.

3.1 Opt-Out & Reset Controls

  • Advertising ID Reset: You may reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time by navigating to your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Complete Local Data Erasure: Because all gameplay data resides on your device, clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local save data.

For further details on how Google processes data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Terms & Conditions of Use

4.1 License to Use

Old & Rusty grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to download and play Aether: Gravity Inversion for personal entertainment. You may not decompile, reverse-engineer, modify, inject unauthorized scripts into, or redistribute the App.

4.2 Virtual Items, Loyalty Tiers & Voucher Codes

  • Virtual Currency & Cosmetics: "Aether Gems", Core shapes (including Hexagon), Color frequencies (including Solar Gold), and Arena Themes (including Bio Abyss) are digital in-game licensed items with no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, transferred, or redeemed for cash.
  • Pilot Loyalty Refill Pricing: Unlocking the Pro Vault or Ultimate Singularity package grants permanent access to discounted Loyalty Refill pricing on that device profile.
  • Single-Use Redemption Codes: Any voucher code generated upon package purchase (formatted OLD-XXXX-XXXX) is cryptographically bound to the purchasing device's local profile ID and may only be redeemed once.

4.3 Anti-Tamper Enforcement

The App utilizes automated cryptographic signature verification (ae_sig). Any attempt to alter local storage balances, bypass billing flows, or forge redemption codes will automatically trigger an integrity reset of tampered values to their default baseline.

5. Fair-Play Advertising Disclosure

At Old & Rusty, we believe intrusive advertising ruins mobile gaming. We enforce a strict, transparent Fair-Play Ad Standard inside Aether: Gravity Inversion:

  • Zero Forced Pop-Up Ads: We never interrupt your flow with unprompted full-screen interstitial video ads between levels or after crashes.
  • 100% Obstruction-Free Active Gameplay: The bottom banner ad is automatically hidden the instant active, unpaused gameplay begins, guaranteeing zero accidental clicks while you tap to invert gravity.
  • Menu & Overlay Placement Only: The 320×50 banner ad is displayed strictly on non-action screens (Home Screen, Pause Menu, and Modal Overlays) with dedicated safe-zone padding separating the banner from all interactive buttons.
  • 100% Opt-In Rewarded Videos: Full-screen video ads only play when you explicitly click a button marked "Watch Ad" (such as reviving to protect your Heart & Win Streak, refilling +3 Hearts, test-driving a locked Shop item for 1 level, doubling a Boss Bounty, or claiming +5 daily Gems).
