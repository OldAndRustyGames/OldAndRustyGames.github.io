### Our Studio Standards
1. **Built for New & Older Hardware:** Engineered with Zero-GC object pooling and lightweight 2.5D canvas rendering for smooth 60 FPS gameplay on low-end and older Android devices.
2. **Fair-Play Monetization:** Zero forced interstitial pop-ups. Banner ads are completely hidden during active gameplay, and all video ads are 100% player-initiated opt-in rewards.
3. **Offline-First & Private:** Zero external tracking servers or account sign-ups required.
